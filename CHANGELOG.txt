## OmniCast Forever v0.9.0 (beta)

First public beta. One bar for whatever your character is doing right now:

- **Casts:** progress, latency, the spell-queue point and the queued spell icon, pushback, and your target.
- **Channels:** a draining bar with tick marks.
- **Global cooldown:** after instants, labelled with the spell you used.
- **Melee swings:** main hand, splitting into main and off hand when dual wielding. The bar follows weapon swaps mid-combat.
- **Next-swing abilities** (Heroic Strike, Cleave, Maul, Raptor Strike): the bar glows and shows the ability.
- **Ranged:**
  - Auto Shot, with its wind-up marked and a "Stop to shoot" warning.
  - Wands, shot by shot while you stand still.
  - Throw and other ranged shots, with their cooldown.
- **Press and Hold Casting:** the held spell shows as next up.

Move and resize the bar in Edit Mode. Open settings with `/oc`, and try `/oc test` for a demo.

**Beta:** played on a warlock, mage, warrior and hunter; other classes are less tested. Please report bugs on GitHub (https://github.com/Popidge/OmniCast_Forever) or in the comments. `/oc log` records a log to attach.
